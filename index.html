Questions

Before you order

What does "research use only" mean?

Our products are sold for laboratory and research purposes only. They're not approved by the FDA for human or animal consumption, and we don't make claims about therapeutic use. It's on the buyer to understand and follow the rules that apply where they are.

How do I pay?

Checkout is by Zelle. Add items to your cart, go to checkout, and you'll get the exact amount, our Zelle number, and an order reference to include in the payment memo. We confirm and ship once payment is received.

How is everything shipped?

Lyophilized (powder) vials ship at room temperature in protective packaging. Bacteriostatic water ships separately if temperature matters for your location — message us if you need cold packaging.

Can I see the COA for my order?

Yes — every order includes the lot number, and we'll send the matching certificate of analysis on request.
